Church Growth & Stats App

A live congregation health dashboard, integrated directly with Planning Center — giving leadership visibility they'd never had before.

The problem

The leadership team had no reliable way to track the health of their congregation over time. Attendance data lived in Planning Center, but there was no way to slice it by location, age group, or discipleship stage — let alone spot trends across months or years. Every question about "how are we actually doing?" meant manually digging through exports, with no easy way to compare across campuses.

What we built

I built a stats dashboard that connects directly to Planning Center, so there's no duplicate data entry and no manual exports. Leaders can now see attendance broken down by location and by age group, and track progress through key milestones — things like Gateway, baptisms, and course completions — all from one live view.

Rather than static numbers, the dashboard surfaces trends and patterns through graphs, so shifts that would have gone unnoticed in a spreadsheet — like a location's attendance quietly declining, or a milestone stalling — are now visible early enough to act on.

Outcomes

Leadership can now see congregation health at a glance across all locations
Attendance trends and patterns visible for the first time
Integrates directly with Planning Center — no double entry

Why it matters

Good decisions need good information. This project turned data that already existed, but was locked away and hard to interpret, into something leadership could actually use to guide where they invest time and resources next.
