Care & Community App

Replacing a single fragile spreadsheet with a proper web app for a community care team — recipients, agencies, deliveries, and events, all in one reliable system.

The problem

A community care team was running their entire operation — recipients, partner agencies, social workers, delivery locations, and scheduling — off a single spreadsheet. It worked, until it didn't: duplicated rows, no backups, no automation, and no way to see the bigger picture. Hours were lost every week just keeping the data intact, time that could have gone toward actually reaching more people.

What we built

I rebuilt the whole operation as a proper web app, starting with clean relational data instead of a flat spreadsheet. From there:

Delivery scheduling became automated, removing the manual coordination that used to eat up hours each week. New agencies and social workers could onboard themselves through dedicated forms, instead of being added manually one at a time. A stats dashboard gave the team a live view of deliveries, recipients, and agency reach at a glance — numbers that used to require digging through spreadsheet tabs to find.

Alongside the core system, the team also gained a group events tool with check-in, making it easy to run and track community events without a separate system bolted on the side.

Outcomes

Hours saved from daily admin — redirected to reaching more people
Delivery process fully automated, response times significantly faster
Replaced a fragile single spreadsheet with a reliable, scalable system

Why it matters

For a team focused on caring for people, time spent wrestling with spreadsheets is time not spent on the actual mission. This project wasn't about flashy features — it was about removing friction, so the people doing the real work could get back to it.
